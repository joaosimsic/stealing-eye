How to make your recordings smaller
These settings usually matter much more than the container:

Format: kvm

- Codec: H.264 for compatibility; H.265/HEVC for smaller files if your playback devices support it.
- Resolution: Record at 1080p rather than 4K if 1080p is sufficient.
- Bitrate: Lower bitrate means smaller files, but too low causes visible quality loss.
- Frame rate: Match the source, commonly 24, 30, or 60 fps.
